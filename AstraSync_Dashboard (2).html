Summary

Recommendation: Option C

AstraSync must select the most favorable contract structure from three available alternatives. This dashboard evaluates each option using discounted revenues and labor costs, the probabilities of earning a bonus or winning the RFP, and the distribution of client savings. It also examines sensitivity to Option B’s bonus amount and Option C’s share of savings. Option C offers the highest expected present-value contribution of $509,768, compared with $506,713 for Option B and $501,522 for Option A. AstraSync should pursue Option C if its risk appetite permits a 60% chance of losing the RFP; otherwise, Option B provides the next-highest expected contribution while securing the project.

Valuation assumptions

All payments and costs occur at month-end. Monthly discount rate is 1.5%; monthly labor cost is $120,000. No incremental bidding costs are specified, so losing the RFP yields zero contribution. Savings are conditional on winning; the stated win probability does not vary with the savings outcome. Expected-value ranking does not adjust for management’s risk preferences.

This HTML dashboard evaluates expected gain-share by numerical integration of the triangular density. The companion R dashboard estimates it by simulation, so its rounded results may differ slightly.

Conclusion

Pursue C, subject to credible probability estimates

C has the highest expected PV at $509,768, but its lead over B is only $3,055. B overtakes C if its bonus exceeds $805,705, or if C’s gain-share falls below 99.05% of current terms, with other inputs held at baseline.

If management prioritizes guaranteed contribution, A offers $501,522 under the stated contract assumptions. Before choosing C, management should confirm that it can accept losing the contract, validate the estimated 40% win probability and protect the negotiated gain-share terms.

All values are net project contribution in present-value dollars. Inputs follow the existing AstraSync model.
